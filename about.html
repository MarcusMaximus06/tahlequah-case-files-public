About this project

Tahlequah Case Files is an independent, non-commercial local-history and public-records research project covering Tahlequah and Cherokee County, Oklahoma.

How statements are labelled

  • CONFIRMED — supported by a primary record or multiple independent sources.
  • REPORTED — published by a news outlet or official statement; attributed, not endorsed.
  • ALLEGED — an accusation or charge. Not a finding of guilt.
  • THEORY — a hypothesis, clearly labelled as such.
  • CONTRADICTED — sources disagree; the conflict is shown, not resolved.
